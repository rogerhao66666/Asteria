# Candidate asset licence audit — 2026-10-16

Source: a ChatGPT-compiled list of 69 candidates. Each page below was fetched
on 2026-10-16 and the licence text read. Nothing was downloaded. Quotes are
from the source page; "not stated" means the page says nothing either way.
Packs not listed here (Kenney items other than those already in
`THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md`, Tiny Swords, Pixel Adventure UI) were **not checked**.

Verdict key: **OK** = page states CC0 (or CC BY with simple credit) and no
extra restriction; may be committed with a `CREDITS.md`. **LOCAL** = usable in
a game, but not allowed in a public Starter Pack / repo. **HOLD** = terms
unreadable or contradictory; do not use until resolved.
