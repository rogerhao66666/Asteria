## Downloaded for preview (2026-10-23, local only, git-ignored)

All under `local_reference/free_assets/`; each pack's licence file was read after download.

- `ravenmore_icons/` - `RavenmoreIconPack.02.2014.zip` (14.1 MB): 41 icons at 64/128/512 px
  plus PSD; 12 base weapon/armour icons each have an upgraded version, and red/green/blue gems.
- `melle_fantasy_swords/` - 7 PNGs (2.2 MB), 408x1419 painted swords: fire, ice, lightning,
  plain, rusty, dagger, and an overview sheet.
- `7soul_496_icons/` - `496_RPG_icons.zip` (1.5 MB): 496 icons at 34x34; 174 are weapons,
  armour, accessories and materials (prefixes W_, A_, Ac_, E_), the rest skills, potions, items.
- `kaykit_adventurers/` - `KayKit_Adventurers_2.0_FREE.zip` (13 MB), `License.txt` says CC0:
  Knight, Barbarian, Mage, Ranger, Rogue, Rogue_Hooded (.glb, rigged, `handslot.l/r` nodes)
  and 31 props (1H/2H swords, axes, shields, bows, crossbows, staff, wand, spellbook).
- `kaykit_character_animations/` - `KayKit_Character_Animations_1.1.zip` (14.9 MB),
  `License.txt` says CC0: 139 Rig_Medium clips, incl. Melee_1H_Attack_Chop / Jump_Chop /
  Slice_Diagonal / Slice_Horizontal / Stab, 2H and dual-wield attacks, Melee_Block*,
  Ranged_Magic_*, Ranged_Bow_*, Hit_A/B, Death_A/B, Idle_A/B, Dodge_*, Jump_*, Cheering.

Rendering the KayKit models into sprite sheets needs Blender (not installed yet).

Still true from 2026-10-22: no finished anime-style walking or battle sprite that may be
redistributed was found. CC0 anime visual-novel portraits exist (see memory note).
